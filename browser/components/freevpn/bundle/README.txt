Free VPN and the built-in ad blocker look here for bundled copies of Tor
(freevpn-tor/tor/tor[.exe]), wireproxy for the WireGuard provider
(freevpn-wireguard/wireproxy[.exe]) and uBlock Origin
(distribution/extensions/uBlock0@raymondhill.net.xpi). See
browser/components/freevpn/README.md in the source tree.
